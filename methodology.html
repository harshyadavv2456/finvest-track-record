Methodology

This site is an automated, deterministic record of what a quantitative model (FinVest) published, measured as hypothetical equal-weight books. It is research/educational. No signal is ever reconstructed from later data, and no price is interpolated or invented.

Data of record

  • Daily snapshot of every scored stock (IN ≈844, US ≈611) via the public API; stored immutably as data/signals/<as_of_date>/<market>.json.gz with fields ticker, intent, conviction, conviction_pct, return_p10/p50/p90, cvar_95, asset_regime, market_regime, last_price, price_date, as_of_date, source, fetched_at_ist.
  • Freshness: as_of_date must equal the expected latest run date (after 14:30 IST: today’s IST date; before: yesterday’s). If older or unchanged → STALE, no day is recorded, the fetcher keeps retrying (30 s, 1 m, 2 m, 5 m, 10 m, then every 15 min until 21:00 IST); then the website UI is probed and the day is marked PENDING and retried from 09:00 IST next day. Identical content on a trading day is also STALE.
  • Prices: Yahoo Finance (adjusted close; adjusted open = open × adj_close/close), re-downloaded each run; unfinished sessions are dropped; cross-checked against FinVest /api/ticker/<t>/daily, differences >1% are logged and flagged.

Timing (no look-ahead)

The observation time is the timestamp at which this tracker fetched the signal (fetched_at_ist) — conservative, since FinVest publishes ≈14:00 IST on the signal date.

Exits use the same price type and the same mapping (a REDUCE/EXIT/AVOID observed at time t is executed at the India close / US open as above). Signal-day open variant (theoretical — not tradeable for India): open of the first session after the as_of_date; shown only for comparison.

A signal day is locked when its entry session has finished (session close + 45 min) and benchmark prices for that session exist. Only locked days are published (SEBI gating); unlocked snapshots stay in private data.

Books

  • Book A (headline): enter when intent is INITIATE/ADD and not held; hold while INITIATE/ADD/HOLD; exit on REDUCE/EXIT/AVOID, or when absent from the scored universe (reason logged; names that failed to fetch on a day are carried, not exited). Equal weight, rebalanced daily to equal weight, no pyramiding, re-entry = new trade, no positions = cash at 0%, marked to market at each close, NAV starts at 100. Same-session events: for US (open) the day return is split into previous-close→open (old book) and open→close (new book).
  • Book B: every signal day, equal-weight all INITIATE+ADD at the tradeable price, hold one session, chain-link.
  • Costs: gross, and net = round trip India 0.25%, US 0.05% (half charged on turnover in Book A, full on each Book-B name).
  • Edge cases: missing price → last close carried and flagged; no bar for 10 consecutive sessions → treated as delisted/no data and exited at the last price (flagged); a name with no price at the entry session is skipped (flagged).

Market indices (three per market) and risk-free

Every comparison can be switched between three indices (the “Compare with” selector; the first is the default). India: NIFTY 500 (^CRSLDX), NIFTY 50 (^NSEI), NIFTY Next 50 (^NSMIDCP). Yahoo has no usable NIFTY Midcap 100 history (the symbol returns a single bar), so Next 50 is used as the third index; this is a documented substitution. US: S&P 500 (SPY), Nasdaq-100 (QQQ), S&P 500 equal-weight (RSP). Prices come from Yahoo (adjusted), are stored in data/prices/indices_<market>.csv.gz and are included in the backup zip. Each series is checked daily for missing sessions and jumps, and cross-checked against an independent Yahoo series (e.g. SPY vs ^GSPC, QQQ vs ^NDX, ^NSEI vs NIFTYBEES) by daily-return correlation (shown on Data health). Indices are price series measured on the same timing as the portfolios. Risk-free: US 3-month T-bill (^IRX, daily = yield/252); India 91-day T-bill: assumed constant 5.5% p.a. (documented assumption).

Stock Explorer

The Stocks page lists every stock in the latest snapshot with the model’s stored rationale (shown in plain words, original text one click away), conviction, regime and its own forecast range. Price moves (1 day, 1 week = 5 sessions, 1 month = 21, 3 months = 63) are real Yahoo adjusted closes up to the latest finished session, each next to the selected index’s move over the same sessions. “Since signal” is the move from the close of the signal date to the latest close and stays “pending” until a session after the signal date has closed. Moves up to the signal date are context, not results. The verdict compares the 3-month move (1-month if shorter) with the index: Beat (at least 2 points above), In line (within 2 points), Lagged (rose but at least 2 points below) or Fell more than the market (fell and at least 2 points below). A long-signal name is published only after its entry session has closed (+45 min); until then only the count and the unlock time are shown.

KPIs and honesty rules

Return, excess, CAGR (only ≥252 days), volatility (ann., √252), max/current drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino (excess over risk-free), beta, information ratio, % days beating, trade hit-rate (positive and beating benchmark over the same window), average win/loss, profit factor, holding sessions, turnover, exposure. Every metric shows its sample size; metrics are labelled not yet meaningful until ≥30 closed trades and ≥60 trading days. Signal quality: forward 1/5/21/63-session returns of locked signal days vs benchmark (long signals, AVOID, HOLD, all), by conviction bucket (<0.5, 0.5–0.6, 0.6–0.7, ≥0.7), by asset regime, and calibration of return_p10/p50/p90 (21-session; ~80% should fall inside p10–p90).
